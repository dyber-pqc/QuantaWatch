       --from-literal=ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-ant-... \
       --from-literal=OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-...

  2. Set gateway.apiKeySecret=quantawatch-api-keys in your values.

  3. Point your AI agent at the gateway proxy URL to start proxying requests.
